How GoalScope picks are made

No tipster hype. We combine fixture sweeps, head-to-head, venue effects and advanced metrics — and show every stat behind every pick.

◈

1. Sweep 60+ fixtures

Verified league and cup feeds covering League Cup, La Liga, Coppa, Eredivisie, Ekstraklasa, Swiss, Scottish and MLB — scanned daily before 09:00 WAT. Every match card shows its kick-off time (WAT).

◎

2. Stat dive per match

Form (last 5), H2H, injuries, rotation risk (cup games), altitude (Coors Field), xG for football, ERA/WHIP and park factors for baseball. No stat = no pick.

◆

3. Choose value markets

We prefer BTTS, Over 2.5/9.5 runs, and Win+Goals over straight 1X2. Each leg stays under ~2.05 — accumulators grow by compounding, not longshots.

✦

4. No duplicates, ever

Multiple slips per day never reuse the same match+market. If no alternative value exists, the slot is left empty rather than repeated. Tracked so it applies every day.

Transparency promise

Every slip lists reasoning, kick-off time (WAT) and indicative odds. Accurate live odds always on sportybet.com — odds shown here may change. We publish losses too — "Lessons Learned" updates daily. Not financial advice. 18+ Gamble responsibly.
