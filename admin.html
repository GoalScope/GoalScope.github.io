How publishing works (public hosting)

  1. Upload here → preview appears instantly (saved in your browser).
  2. To make it public for everyone: push the files in predictions-website/data/ to GitHub, then deploy to Vercel/Netlify (see README).
  3. Or run the sync script on your phone: it copies /storage/emulated/0/Predictions/*.txt into the site automatically.
